Result Interpretation
  • ASM: Allele-Specific Methylation (as est. by methbat).
  • ASM Range (35-65%): Evaluated directly from the meth score (bulk aggregate methylation). Normal monoallelic ASM is expected between 0.35–0.65 (35–65%). Out-of-range loci are flagged (<35% Hypo [Blue] or >65% Hyper [Red]) and their visualization bars are dimmed.
  • Meth Delta: Difference in methylation between phased haplotypes (Haplotype 1 − Haplotype 2). Values near ±1.0 indicate strong allele-specific asymmetry; values near 0.0 indicate symmetric biallelic methylation or unmethylation.
  • G (gnomAD): External link to gnomAD v4 Browser.
  • N (NCBI): External link to NCBI Genome Data Viewer.
  • QC PASS: Both report_summary and qc_warn are 'PASS' (Green text).
  • QC Warning: Either report_summary or qc_warn is not 'PASS' (Orange text).
  • QC Failed: Both report_summary and qc_warn are not 'PASS' (Red text).
  • meth score: Overall bulk methylated fraction (mean_combined_methyl, 0.0 to 1.0).
  • unmeth score: Overall bulk unmethylated fraction (1 - meth score).

Methods and Context

This report summarizes hg38 methylation loci identified by the gsm-nallo pipeline. Methylation is phased using HiPhase and visualized using IGV-Web.

Required Files
  • *methbat.csv methbat results
  • *haplotagged.bam & .bai (Phased Reads)
  • *combined.bw (Total Methylation Signal)
  • *hap1.bw / *hap2.bw (Allele-Specific Tracks)
